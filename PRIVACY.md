# Scrollkeeper Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Scrollkeeper does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **Your books stay on your devices.** A book you pick is converted on your iPhone and sent to your own Apple Watch over Apple's built-in connection between the two. It is never uploaded anywhere.
- **Text recognition happens on your iPhone.** Scanned PDF pages are read with Apple's on-device text recognition. No page or text leaves the phone.
- **No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.** The apps contain no third-party code and make no network connections.
- **What is saved.** Your books, reading positions, bookmarks, text size and scroll speed are saved on your Apple Watch so the app can show them again. The watch tells your own iPhone which books it has and how far you've read them, so the iPhone app can list them. Deleting a book removes its position and bookmarks; deleting the app removes everything.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page.
